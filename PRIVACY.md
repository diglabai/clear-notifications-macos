# Privacy

Clear Notifications is local-only software.

- No analytics, telemetry, advertising, accounts, or network requests.
- No notification titles, message text, sender names, or app content are stored or logged.
- Accessibility access is used only to find exposed notification dismissal controls and activate their close action.
- The process exits after the clear operation completes.

The `--dry-run --no-open` command reports only Accessibility availability and a numeric target count.
