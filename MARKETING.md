# Launch copy

## Tagline

Clear Notification Center in one click.

## One-line description

A tiny, local-only macOS Dock app that dismisses visible notifications and exits—no account, tracking, or background service.

## Short launch post

Notification Center gets noisy fast. Clear Notifications gives your Mac a simple one-click reset from the Dock. It runs locally, clears visible alerts, and exits. No login, telemetry, subscription, or always-running helper.

## Product Hunt subtitle

One-click Notification Center cleanup for macOS, with zero tracking.

## Community post

I wanted a fast way to clear a crowded macOS Notification Center without disabling future alerts. Clear Notifications is a small open-source Dock app that uses the standard Accessibility permission, dismisses visible cards, and immediately exits. The public beta is source-first while notarized binary distribution is prepared. Feedback on additional macOS versions is welcome.

## Key claims

- One click from the Dock.
- Local-only operation.
- No notification-content logging.
- No account, tracking, subscription, or background service.
- Open source under the MIT License.
